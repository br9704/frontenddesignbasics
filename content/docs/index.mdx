<Banner name="readme" alt="Frontend Design Basics: complete, beautiful web design" />

Most sites that look "clean" are really just empty. The ones people remember are **complete**: they
have a point of view, the small details have been designed, the motion means something, and they still
work on a phone with reduced motion switched on.

This guide collects what makes that difference, in one place, in a form you can use today.

## How every page works

<Steps>

## Use it with an agent

The whole guide is published as [`/llms.txt`](/llms.txt) and the toolkit is a single
[`toolkit.json`](https://github.com/br9704/frontenddesignbasics/blob/main/toolkit/toolkit.json).
Point your coding agent at the repo when you start a project and ask *"what in here fits this brief?"*

## Use a 4pt scale

<SpacingRhythm />

```css
:root {
  --space-1: 4px;  --space-2: 8px;  --space-3: 12px; --space-4: 16px;
  --space-6: 24px; --space-8: 32px; --space-10: 40px; --space-16: 64px; --space-24: 96px;
}
```

Tailwind's default spacing is already a 4pt scale. Use it, and don't reach for arbitrary values like
`mt-[13px]`.

## Group by proximity

Related things sit close; unrelated things sit far apart. The gap *between* groups should be clearly
bigger (about 2×) than the gap *within* them. This does more for structure than any border or card.

## Layout rules that hold up

- **Container:** max width around 1200–1400px for pages and 65ch for text.
- **Gutters:** at least 16px on phones. Test at **390px** and check nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Asymmetry is allowed.** A 1:2 split (like this site's contents section) feels more designed than
  three equal columns.
- **Sections breathe:** 80–120px of vertical space between major sections on desktop.

## Why it works

Space is the cheapest luxury. Generous, consistent spacing is what makes an expensive brand look
expensive, and it costs nothing but restraint.

Style changes every few years. The principles underneath do not. A brutalist site and a soft pastel
one can both be excellent, and they are excellent for the same reasons.

## The five that matter most

1. **One point of view.** Decide what the thing *is* before deciding what it looks like. A coffee
   roaster, a banking dashboard and a game studio should never share a template.
2. **A scale for everything.** Type sizes, spacing and radii come from a scale, not from the moment.
   Scales are why good sites feel calm. See [Typography](/docs/principles/typography) and
   [Space & layout](/docs/principles/space-and-layout).
3. **One accent, used with intent.** Colour that means something ("this is interactive", "this is
   the one number that matters") beats colour as decoration. See [Colour](/docs/principles/colour).
4. **Hierarchy by contrast, not by quantity.** One big thing per view. Size, weight, colour and space
   do the ranking; boxes and borders are the last resort.
5. **The details are the design.** Focus states, empty states, loading, errors, hover, the 390px phone
   and reduced motion. [The rules](/docs/principles/rules) turns this into a checklist.
